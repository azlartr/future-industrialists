The Idea

The Future Industrialists Initiative

The first of its kind in social responsibility in the industrial sector, and one of the initiatives of the Distinguished Initiatives Association.

The initiative is a project that targets students in public and university education, opening a new window for them to discover the industrial strength of the Kingdom, in line with the direction of Saudi Vision 2030 to diversify income sources by raising industrial output.

The industrial sector is the core pillar of sustainable economic development. Through this program, together with our partners, we aim to motivate our male and female students to work in the industrial field and to open a partnership gateway with the private sector to qualify them and provide them with full support.

Where We Are Heading

Initiative Goals

Five goals that meet at one point: a national generation that sees industry from the inside.

Building passion among male and female students to join the industrial field in the future, and giving them the chance to see the inner workings of the industrial community.

Activating the role of the non-profit sector in supporting Saudi Vision 2030 and contributing to national development.

Launching a distinctive initiative through the Association that maximizes the Kingdom’s economic impact.

Achieving Saudi Vision 2030 by transforming the Kingdom into a leading industrial power.

Delivering a distinctive, sustainable program for public education students that builds human capabilities for the future.

Message from the General Supervisor

A Nation That Builds

“We aspire for this initiative to be the first of its kind to leave a mark on the industrial community in the Kingdom.”

Today, the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia has tremendous industrial wealth, and it has a distinctive strategic location linking the three continents of the world, where His Royal Highness Prince Mohammed bin Salman launched the National Industrial Strategy within the programs of Saudi Vision 2030. From here, the idea of the Future Industrialists initiative emerged to be a distinctive partnership between the government, non-profit and industrial sectors to deliver a distinctive program for our male and female students in partnership with the Ministry of Industry and Mineral Resources and the Ministry of Education, and to open horizons for our youth to become among the industry leaders of the Kingdom.

Dr. Muhannad bin Naeem Al-SheikhBoard Member · General Supervisor of the Future Industrialists Initiative
